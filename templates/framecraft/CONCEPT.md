# Small spark, big world

An ink dot draws its own universe and blossoms into a printed poster: one small
mark can become something rich. The dot remains the center throughout.

- 0.00–0.30: textured poster and one ink spark; a short pluck marks its arrival.
- 0.30–0.85: an orbit draws outward from the mark, with a soft scratch/whoosh.
- 0.85–1.85: cyan, ochre and rose ink petals unfold in successive layers; staggered plucks follow the build.
- 1.85–2.46: striped satellites and tiny sparks complete the world; bell and riser.
- 2.46–2.66: motion slows and the score drops to a brief breath.
- 2.70–3.00: the bloom expands, seed points settle, and the closing line arrives with a warm impact and chord.
- 3.00–4.00: closing poster holds: “Small spark. Big world.” and “Paper, ink & possibility.”

The camera stays locked. Every mark emerges from the original center or its
orbital drawing. A shared master timeline drives both the Canvas scene and the
48 kHz stereo synthesized score. Audio is inspected numerically, not auditioned.
