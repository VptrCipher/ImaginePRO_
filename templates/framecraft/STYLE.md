# Framecraft showcase look

The supplied Framecraft.md is retained byte for byte. This adaptation uses its
handmade print direction; no reference images were supplied or imitated.

Palette: paper #f3eddf; deep blue ink #172f3b; rose #df6580; teal #3996a4;
ochre #e8ad3d; vermilion #d8643d; construction lines #d6cbb6; resist #fff9e9.
Cached paper mottling, fibres, pigment specks, diagonal halftone, slightly offset
ink plates, stepped contour wobble and four grain frames supply the texture.

Locked portrait poster: editorial serif header, full-width subject band, a ruled
foreground and a closing headline above the bottom 250-pixel safe area. Georgia
and Segoe UI use installed system fonts. Color builds in staggered plates with
overshoot; orbital secondary motion settles at the payoff. The same ink dot
becomes the center of the flower/universe.

Do not copy: the document's jellyfish, owl, wheel, city, waterfall, koi, horse,
characters, science scenes, quoted example scripts or any studio branding. The
original scene here is an abstract printed bloom. No factual claims are made.
